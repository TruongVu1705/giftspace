## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, nocturnal luxury tailored for digital creators, creative technologists, and curated asset curators. It fuses dark minimalism with an intoxicating, rose-tinted palette—evoking the intimacy of a dimly lit atelier combined with the precision of high-end developer and designer tools.

### Brand Personality
- **Sensual Precision:** Technical rigor softened by deep, romantic undertones.
- **Curated Prestige:** Premium craftsmanship that feels deliberate, exclusive, and distilled.
- **Architectural Poise:** Clean grid lines, disciplined geometry, and focused high-contrast focal points.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bespoke hybrid of **Dark Minimalism** and **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Structural integrity is achieved through low-contrast rose borders and deep obsidian-wine tonal tiers, energized by tactical strikes of glowing neon ruby. The visual cadence remains uncluttered, confident, and deeply immersive.

## Colors

The palette leverages obsidian undertones steeped in dried rose petals, anchored by luminous ruby accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#120e10`):** Ultra-deep rose charcoal. The foundational infinite void.
- **Surfaces:**
  - `surface-base`: `#120e10` (Root viewport)
  - `surface-sunken`: `#0d0a0b` (Recessed wells, terminal panes)
  - `surface-container-low`: `#1a1418` (Primary cards, asset cards, sidebars)
  - `surface-container-high`: `#231a20` (Modals, hover states, elevated popovers)
- **Borders & Framing:**
  - `border-subtle`: `#381e28` (Structural grid dividers, quiet separators)
  - `border-bold`: `#4a2835` (Active card strokes, hover outlines, input perimeters)
- **Accents:**
  - `primary`: `#f43f5e` (Vibrant rosy crimson for primary CTAs, active badges, status indicators)
  - `primary-hover`: `#fb7185` (Luminous rose highlight state)
  - `primary-active`: `#e11d48` (Pressed and deepened dynamic feedback)
- **Typography & Foreground:**
  - `text-primary`: `#fff0f2` (Soft ivory tinted with rose for absolute clarity without harsh optical glare)
  - `text-secondary`: `#f5d0d6` (Muted blush for supportive metadata, subheadings, labels)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#c4a5ad` (Dampened dusty rose for placeholders, disabled states, and timestamps)

## Typography

The typography couples the sharp, geometric intrigue of **Space Grotesk** with the clean, fluid readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**, reinforced by **JetBrains Mono** for technical code assets and metadata.

### Hierarchical Roles
- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk provides a tech-forward yet sculptural presence. Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates a high-density, editorial posture.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers warm, effortless legibility across large swaths of text and metadata lists.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Capitalized, tracked-out Space Grotesk conveys deliberate precision in pills, tabs, and technical specifications.
- **Monospace Code:** JetBrains Mono serves asset tokens, hashes, code snippets, and coordinate tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid enforces visual order and rhythm.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, generous outer margins of 3rem (`48px`). Content container caps at 1440px width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.25rem (`20px`) margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Layout relies on a base-4 spatial matrix. Internal component packaging adheres to `space-xs` through `space-md`, while section cadences rely on `space-lg` to `space-xl` to prevent visual clutter against dark, saturated surfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal stacking** complemented by **tinted ambient glows** and **delicate rose border filaments**, strictly avoiding standard muddy black drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Root Base):** Background at `#120e10`. Zero elevation.
2. **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Canvas Grid):** Solid `#1a1418` enveloped by a crisp 1px border of `#381e28`.
3. **Tier 2 (Elevated Sheets & Floating Panels):** `#231a20` backed by an ethereal ambient glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.08)`. Border lifts to `#4a2835`.
4. **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Dialogs):** `#231a20` with 85% opacity blended through a `20px` backdrop-filter blur. External edge highlighted with `0 0 0 1px #4a2835`, grounded by a deep plum-tinted drop: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(18, 14, 16, 0.9)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, prioritizing architectural structure over playful roundness.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components:** 4px (`0.25rem`) for buttons, text fields, menu items, and code blocks.
- **Containers:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for asset cards, preview frames, and drawer segments.
- **Modals & Hero Elements:** 12px (`0.75rem`) for heavy floating overlays.
- **Strict Exception:** Status pills and micro-chips leverage pill geometry (`9999px`) to visually separate metadata tokens from structural interactive containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Neon ruby background (`#f43f5e`), ivory text (`#fff0f2`), font-weight 600. On hover: shifts to `#fb7185` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.35)`). Active: `#e11d48`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border in `#4a2835`. Text in `#f5d0d6`. On hover: surface transitions to `#231a20`, border brightens to `#fb7185`, and text turns to `#fff0f2`.

### Cards & Asset Previews
- **Container:** `#1a1418` surface framed by a 1px `#381e28` border.
- **Hover State:** Card rises smoothly (`translateY(-2px)`), border intensifies to `#4a2835`, and a delicate ruby rim glow is cast (`0 6px 24px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.06)`).
- **Asset Meta:** High-contrast tags with JetBrains Mono, rendered on sunken `#0d0a0b` badge backgrounds.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Base:** `#120e10` background, border in `#381e28`, text in `#fff0f2`, placeholder in `#c4a5ad`.
- **Focus:** Border snaps to `#f43f5e` with an internal focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #f43f5e`). Accompanying iconography activates from `#c4a5ad` to `#fb7185`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default:** Background in `#1a1418`, 1px border in `#381e28`, uppercase typography (`label-sm`) in `#c4a5ad`.
- **Selected:** Background `#4a2835`, border `#f43f5e`, text `#fff0f2`. A small glowing ruby dot precedes the chip label.

### Lists & Tables
- **Rows:** Alternating rows utilize subtle transitions from `#120e10` to `#1a1418`. Dividers use a razor-thin 1px line in `#381e28`.
- **Row Hover:** Illumination to `#231a20` with a persistent left-edge neon ruby accent notch (`3px` width).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** Inset border of 1.5px in `#4a2835` over a `#120e10` interior.
- **Checked:** Background in `#f43f5e`, border `#f43f5e`, inner glyph rendered in pure `#fff0f2`.

### Specialized Asset Hub Component: Code Snippet Panes
- Recessed background `#0d0a0b` with border `#381e28`. Top action bar features copy triggers in `#c4a5ad` that burst into `#fb7185` with micro-haptic text changes upon execution.
